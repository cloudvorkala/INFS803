TESTING SUMMARY

Functional testing was carried out on the Paperbound Online Bookstore application to verify that the main user-facing features were working according to the expected behaviour. A total of 16 test cases were completed during the testing process. The testing covered key functions such as browsing book categories, filtering books, searching by book title, sorting books by different options, signing in and signing out, adding books to the cart, removing items from the cart, updating cart quantities, checking price calculations, navigating between catalogue pages, and browsing specific categories such as History and Art & Design.

During the testing, the actual results were compared with the expected results for each test case. The search function successfully returned matching books, while category filters displayed books belonging to the selected category. Sorting functions such as Price Low to High, Price High to Low, Newest First, and Title A–Z produced the expected ordering of books. Cart-related functions were also tested successfully, including adding a book, changing the quantity, recalculating the subtotal and total price, and removing an item from the cart. User authentication functions such as sign in and sign out also behaved as expected.

All 16 recorded functional test cases produced the expected results and were marked as PASS. Evidence was recorded for the tested functions to support the testing results. Overall, the testing confirmed that the major functional features examined during this testing session were operating correctly in the local development environment.


CONCLUSION

The functional testing of the Paperbound Online Bookstore application was successfully completed. The results indicate that the main features tested were stable and performed according to their expected requirements. Important user activities, including searching and browsing books, filtering and sorting catalogue items, using authentication features, managing items in the shopping cart, and navigating through catalogue pages, were completed successfully without identifying a functional failure in the recorded test cases.

Based on the 16 completed test cases, the application demonstrated satisfactory functional behaviour for the areas included within the scope of this testing. However, these results apply only to the functions and scenarios that were tested and do not guarantee that the application is free from all possible defects. Further testing could include additional negative test cases, boundary conditions, performance testing, security testing, compatibility testing, and testing against the deployed cloud environment.
